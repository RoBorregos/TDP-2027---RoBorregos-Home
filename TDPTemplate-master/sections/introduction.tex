\section{Introduction}
RoBorregos is the robotics team of Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico, and has competed in the RoboCup@Home OPL since 2023. This paper describes our robot FRIDA (Fig.~\ref{fig:frida}), with emphasis on the work done since our 2026 team description paper. The team, documentation and development logs are available on our website.\footnote{\url{https://athome.roborregos.com/}}
