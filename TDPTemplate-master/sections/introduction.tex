\section{Introduction}
RoBorregos is the undergraduate robotics team of Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico, competing in the @Home OPL since 2023. Its robot, FRIDA (Friendly Robotic Interactive Domestic Assistant), is a domestic service robot with one arm and a custom omnidirectional base. This paper describes the current system, with emphasis on the work done since the 2026 team description paper. The team, documentation and development logs are available on our website.\footnote{\url{https://athome.roborregos.com/}}
