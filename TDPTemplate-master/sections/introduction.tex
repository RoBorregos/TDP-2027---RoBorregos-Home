\section{Introduction}
RoBorregos is the undergraduate robotics team of Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico, competing in the @Home OPL since 2023. Its robot, FRIDA (Friendly Robotic Interactive Domestic Assistant), is a domestic service robot with one arm and an custom omnidirectional base; a photo and its hardware are given in the annex (Fig.~\ref{fig:frida}). This paper describes the current system, with emphasis on the work done since the 2026 team description paper. The team, documentation and development logs are available on our website.\footnote{\url{https://athome.roborregos.com/}}
