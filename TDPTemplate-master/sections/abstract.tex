\begin{abstract}
This paper describes FRIDA (Friendly Robotic Interactive Domestic Assistant), the RoboCup@Home Open Platform League robot of team RoBorregos, and the work done since our 2026 team description paper. The main contributions are: (1) an open-source omnidirectional mecanum base; (2) a multi-command scheduler that merges several GPSR commands into one plan with a Held-Karp search; (3) a geometric grasp generator that fits a box, cylinder or sphere to each object and ranks collision-free approaches; (4) a object-recognition pipeline used to train a 30-class detector in 5 hours at RoboCup 2026; and (5) a low-cost, open-source based soft gripper 3D-printed in TPU, Software is open source at \url{https://github.com/RoBorregos/home2}.
\end{abstract}
