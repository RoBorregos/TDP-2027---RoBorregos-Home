\begin{abstract}
This paper describes FRIDA (Friendly Robotic Interactive Domestic Assistant), the RoboCup@Home Open Platform League robot of team RoBorregos, and the work done since our 2026 team description paper. The main contributions are: (1) an open-source omnidirectional mecanum base that replaces the differential-drive base used until 2026; (2) a multi-command scheduler that merges several GPSR commands into one plan with a Held-Karp search constrained by command order and by carrying one object at a time, reducing driven distance by 39\%; and (3) a geometric grasp generator that fits a box, cylinder or sphere to each object and ranks collision-free approaches, with GPD as a fallback. The paper also summarizes placing with spatial suitability heatmaps, and the object-recognition pipeline used to train a 30-class detector in 5 hours at RoboCup 2026. Code and hardware designs are open source at \url{https://github.com/RoBorregos}.
\end{abstract}
