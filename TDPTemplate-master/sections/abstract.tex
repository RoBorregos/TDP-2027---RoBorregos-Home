\begin{abstract}
This paper introduces FRIDA (Friendly Robotic Interactive Domestic Assistant), a General Purpose Service Robot developed for RoboCup@Home 2027. The system employs an architecture that integrates high-level reasoning with robust low-level actuation. The five main contributions are: (1) A clutter-aware placing strategy that optimizes target poses via spatial suitability heatmaps (95\% success rate in object-placement tasks); (2) A multi-stage interpretation pipeline employing schema-aligned parsing to bridge natural language and physical execution; (3) A low-cost, open-source gripper with fractal structure soft fingertips designed for adaptive grasping; (4) An open-source omnidirectional mecanum base with on-board kinematics and odometry fusion, providing full holonomic mobility; and (5) A multi-command scheduler that merges consecutive GPSR commands into a single plan minimizing Nav2 path length (39\% less driving in simulation). To support the community, the architecture, core modules and ROS 2 workspaces are open-sourced and available at: https://github.com/RoBorregos/home2.
\end{abstract}
