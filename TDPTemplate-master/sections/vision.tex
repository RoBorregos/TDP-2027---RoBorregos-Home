\subsection{Computer Vision}

\subsubsection{Person Perception.}
Faces are detected and encoded with InsightFace~\cite{arcface}, and their embeddings are matched against the gallery of known guests by cosine similarity. Body pose comes from YOLO11-pose, which estimates keypoints for all people in one pass. Gestures and postures are heuristically inferred from joint angles. For open-ended attributes such as clothing are inferred by querying Moondream2.

\subsubsection{Person Following.}
YOLOv8 with ByteTrack~\cite{bytetrack} tracks the followed person frame by frame. Because ByteTrack uses no appearance information, the identity is lost after occlusions, so a Swin Transformer~\cite{swin} Re-ID model trained on Market-1501 recovers it. While the target is visible, an embedding is stored every 0.3\,s in a rolling gallery of up to 128 vectors; when the track is lost, visible people are matched against this gallery by cosine similarity, and the best match above 0.6 is re-acquired as the target. Re-ID runs asynchronously so it never blocks the tracking loop. People with similar clothing remain the main failure case.

\subsubsection{Object Recognition.}
A self-hosted web pipeline\footnote{\url{https://github.com/RoBorregos/home-pipelines}} replaces manual labeling: from a 20-second phone video per object, GroundingDINO~\cite{grounding_dino} localizes the object by class name, SAM3~\cite{sam3} refines its mask, and the crops are composited onto real backgrounds to train YOLO on two NVIDIA A6000 GPUs. At RoboCup 2026, we recorded, trained and deployed a 30-class detector in 5 hours with a single operator, reaching 97.76\% mAP@0.5:0.95 on validation. For objects announced shortly before a test, a path without retraining uses 20 photos per class to embed and store; in runtime, YOLOE~\cite{yoloe} proposes class-agnostic boxes and DINOv2~\cite{dinov2} embeddings are matched against the per-class gallery, reaching 92.2\% proposal recall and 80.6\% gated recall@1, lower than the supervised detector but available within minutes.
