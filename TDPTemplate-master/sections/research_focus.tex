\section{Research focus and interests}
Our research targets the gap between what a domestic robot plans and what it reliably executes. This cycle focused on:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Grasping in clutter: fitted geometric primitives against a point-cloud grasp detector (Table~\ref{tab:manipulation_results}).
\item Multi-command planning: merging GPSR commands into one executable plan (Sec.~\ref{sec:scheduling}), on top of our LLM interpreter published at MICAI~\cite{10.1007/978-3-032-09037-9_24}.
\item Fast object onboarding: training and deploying detectors for new objects at the venue.
\item Holonomic mobility: an open-source mecanum base whose firmware keeps odometry and stops safely independently of ROS (Sec.~\ref{sec:omnibase}).
\item Semantic navigation: planning where the robot must stand to inspect each piece of furniture within the detector's range, and tracking when each was last seen. 
\end{enumerate}
