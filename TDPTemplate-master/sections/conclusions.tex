\section{Conclusions and future work}

This cycle FRIDA gained primitive-based grasping in clutter, multi-command GPSR planning, venue detector training and an open-source omnidirectional base, whose wear at RoboCup 2026 calls for endurance testing and a sturdier drivetrain. A single arm rules out two-handed tasks such as folding clothes, and the end-effector camera gives no visual feedback once the arm moves. Our next platform, now in development, adds a second arm and a fixed head camera keeping grippers and object in view, enabling closed-loop grasping and demonstrations to train Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies by imitation learning.
