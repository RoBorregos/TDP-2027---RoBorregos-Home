\section{Conclusions and future work}

FRIDA integrates perception, manipulation, navigation and HRI modules in a layered architecture. The main lesson from RoboCup 2026 concerned the omnidirectional base: it was new and lightly tested, and sustained use during the competition caused significant mechanical and electronic wear, so longer endurance testing and a more durable drivetrain are priorities. Other limitations include two-handed tasks such as lifting a laundry basket or folding clothes, imposed by a single arm with limited reach and an on-arm camera, motivating future work on a bimanual platform, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, imitation learning, and a static camera for real-time feedback.
